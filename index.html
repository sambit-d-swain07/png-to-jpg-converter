Why Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG and JPG are both image file formats, but they are suited for different purposes. PNG supports transparency and is ideal for web graphics and logos, but it is not as efficient for large photo storage or printing.

JPG files, in contrast, are compressed, smaller in size, and widely accepted for web uploads, printing, and sharing. They don’t support transparency, but they are perfect for:

  • Social media and web uploads
  • Email attachments
  • CMYK print compatibility
  • Better performance on websites
